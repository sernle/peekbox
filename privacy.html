Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

Peekbox does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Your files stay on your Mac

Peekbox opens, previews, extracts and creates archives entirely on your Mac. It makes no network connections: no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting to us, no accounts, and no advertising.

Passwords

If you choose “Remember in Keychain” when unlocking an archive, the password is saved in your own macOS Keychain, where only Peekbox on your Mac can read it. You can delete saved passwords in Peekbox's Settings.

Folder access

macOS asks you before Peekbox may use a folder (for example, to extract files next to an archive). Peekbox remembers your answer on your Mac only; you can review and remove these permissions in Peekbox's Settings.

Purchases

When you buy Peekbox on the Mac App Store, Apple processes the payment under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your payment details.
